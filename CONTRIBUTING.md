# Contributions

Every correction needs a source URL and exact publication/page/line/part locator.
Distinguish direct primary-source inspection from secondhand reporting and new
interpretations. Preserve source uncertainty and label competing classifications.
Never transfer a Greek gloss to an Eteocypriot component merely because the entry
is bilingual. Do not copy restricted scans or images without permission.

Do not edit raw XML to agree with a proposal. Preserve the original response and
add explicitly versioned transformations or source assertions. Rebuild, validate,
test and regenerate exports before publishing. A new source snapshot requires
new provenance and a new release; do not silently replace immutable evidence.

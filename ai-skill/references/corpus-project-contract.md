# Corpus Project AI Research Contract — 0.3

The repository corpus is authoritative. The AI layer is a rebuildable research interface, not a second corpus.

## Evidence contract
Separate observation/source record, normalized or derived representation, computational result, scholarly interpretation, and AI-derived analysis. Missing evidence is unknown or blocked, never filled by inference.

## Provenance and citation
Prefer stable record/claim IDs, repository paths, and source locators. Preserve evidence hashes and source lineage. Multiple digital copies of one upstream witness are not independent witnesses.

## Rights
Consult indexed rights/NOTICE artifacts before reproducing source-derived material. Transformation does not broaden upstream rights.

## Cross-corpus safety
Interoperability permits comparison, not equivalence. Similar signs, strings, positions, or statistics do not alone establish common values, language relationship, borrowing, morphology, decipherment, or independent replication.

## Staleness
Read generated/source-state.json. If its source commit does not correspond to the corpus state being analyzed, report the bundle as stale and use canonical files until regenerated.

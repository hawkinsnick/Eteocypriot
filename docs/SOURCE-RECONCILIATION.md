## Source differences worth reviewing

- **Oxford provenance:** Masson 1953 p. 83 regards Amathus origin of historical 1/2 as unassured and reports absent museum provenance. The IG candidates 3/4 report Amathus and Polemidhia/Oxford. Preserve both source statements and their dates.
- **Grouped Louvre inventory:** the current AM 799 notice groups ICS 194–195; historical 3/4 occupy different parts of the inscribed lintel. Candidate IG 5/6 remain distinct digital entries. A grouped inventory is not two independently verified objects.
- **Possible lost bilingual:** Masson reports tentative disappearance of historical 5 after its 1914 discovery; candidate IG 7 is supported by the Greek parallel, not by verified present-day custody.
- **Historical label 7:** Masson 1953 p. 87 discusses three versus five lines in separate copies. No native entry is attached merely by number equality.
- **Critical readings:** six selected questions retain differences in syllabic readings, word dividers, vanished forms, proposed engraver errors and supplements. Historical sign ordinals are not automatically modern token offsets. See the exact locators in `research/historical-concordance.json`.

The Louvre JSON modification date and displayed notice-update date can differ. They describe catalogue metadata, not the date of the ancient object, the date of a new reading or an independent expert approval.

## Inspection scope and replay

The two historical articles have 32 acquired advertised page views: six text pages plus four supplementary views in 1953, and twenty text pages plus two supplementary views in 1957. Thirteen selected text-page images were visually inspected for metadata: 1953 pp. 83, 84, 87; 1957 pp. 61–70. Other page views are acquired without a completed visual metadata-inspection claim. The ledger uses unique page-view URLs so a supplementary plate cannot overwrite a text-page identity. No continuous historical readings, scans or museum JSON/images are redistributed.

Run `python scripts/audit_source_concordance.py --check` and `python scripts/release_check.py`. The audit checks source replay, correspondence namespaces, selected page locators and admission limits; the release check also runs regression tests and export verification.

Sources: [Masson 1953](https://www.persee.fr/doc/syria_0039-7946_1953_num_30_1_4957), [Masson 1957](https://www.persee.fr/doc/syria_0039-7946_1957_num_34_1_5277), [Louvre AM 1857](https://collections.louvre.fr/ark:/53355/cl010130777), [Louvre AM 799](https://collections.louvre.fr/ark:/53355/cl010134473). The Perna 2018 route returned a bot challenge and the British Museum gem page returned HTTP 403; these are recorded access dependencies, not inspected article/object pages.
